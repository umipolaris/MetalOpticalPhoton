# B2 Rayleigh "blue sky / red sunset" colour demo — GPU (BEAM kernel).
# White continuous beam (1.65-3.10 eV) along +z through a Rayleigh box (n=1, no refraction,
# RAYLEIGH length proportional to lambda^4). Blue (short lambda) scatters out strongly into a
# side halo; red (long lambda) transmits, so the forward beam reddens with depth.
